# Goals and stakeholders

The teaching scenario concerns returning loaned equipment. The borrower or staff
member initiating a return needs an observable outcome; the organization needs the
loan record to reflect that outcome. The maintained feature and port specs define
the actual baseline obligations. This fixture does not claim an approved product
strategy or identify a real stakeholder authority.

The [active change](../changes/record-return-condition/proposal.md) proposes recording
the condition of new returns. That proposed outcome is not a baseline guarantee.
