## Reading decisions

Decision acceptance means a choice was made through the project's decision process;
it does not establish implemented, deployed, or tested behavior. Follow current
architecture views to understand today's design. Retain superseded records and their
reasoning, with explicit links to replacements. Do not copy their rationale here.
