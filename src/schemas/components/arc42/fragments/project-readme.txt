The optional arc42 component supplies a system-wide architecture handbook and
navigation starters under openspec/system-architecture/. Local views remain with
their responsible owners; the handbook links scoped ADRs through its decision index.
Use only sections that help readers understand the system. Planning describes changes
and optional drafts inside the change; Apply delivers mapped descriptive handbook
pages, navigation, and diagrams in the development checkout. Drafts alone are not
handbook delivery. Local-view proposals for openspec/specs/** stay inside the change
through Apply; publication and dependent links follow separately authorized sync/archive-stage
integration. Checks and acceptance remain separate; native requirement sync does not
publish local views, the handbook, or ADRs, nor does a handbook edit deploy a site.
